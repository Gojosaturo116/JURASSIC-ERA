# JURASSIC-ERA
about dinosaurs
 giganatosaurus was a massive meat-eating dinosaur that lived in Argentina about 99 to 95 million years ago.
Key Facts

• Name Meaning: "Giant southern lizard".
• Size: Reached lengths of 12 to 13 meters (39 to 43 feet) and weighed around 6 to 8 tons. It was slightly longer than Tyrannosaurus rex, though generally more slender.
• Diet: Carnivore with 76 long, knife-like, serrated teeth designed for slicing flesh rather than crushing bones.
• Habitat: Lived in the Patagonian region of Patagonia, Argentina, during the Late Cretaceous period.
• Discovery: Unearthed by amateur fossil hunter Rubén Carolini in 1993.
